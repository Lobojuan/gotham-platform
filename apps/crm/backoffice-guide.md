# Gotham Consult back-office quick guide

This guide documents current CRM navigation and actions. It is for signed-in staff and administrators. The interface may hide screens that the current user's role cannot access.

## Navigation

- On desktop, use the left-hand navigation. On a phone, use **More** in the bottom bar to open the full menu.
- **Dashboard** is the starting point. **Calendar**, **Tasks**, and **Team chat** are available to all signed-in roles.
- Staff accounts have limited access. Administrator-only screens include contacts, pipeline, invoices, reports, events, email, data health, and user management. A missing menu item may be a permission restriction; ask an administrator rather than trying to bypass it.

## Add and manage users

- Only the **owner** can create, change, disable, or remove accounts. Go to **System → Users → Add user**, enter the person's name and sign-in name, and choose the least-privileged suitable role.
- The system shows a one-time password after creation or reset. Share it privately. The user must choose a new password at first sign-in. Do not post passwords or sign-in codes in team chat or ask the AI to handle them.
- **Admin** can access operational screens and settings. **Staff** has limited access. **Owner** has full access, including user administration. Do not promote a user merely to make a hidden screen appear.

## Companies and people

- Administrators can open **Sales → Companies & people → Add company or person** to create a record. Enter only information needed for the relationship and confirm the details with the client.
- Open a record to review its notes, tasks, and related deals. Add a dated note after a meaningful call, meeting, email, or WhatsApp exchange, and record the agreed next action as a task.
- Do not put passwords, MoMo PINs, card details, or unnecessary sensitive information in notes.

## Tasks and deals

- Use **Tasks → New task** to assign a clear action and due date. Keep the title specific, such as “Confirm product sales channels with client”. Mark it complete when done.
- Administrators can create a deal in **Sales → Pipeline**. Update its stage and next step after each material client interaction. Use a value only when the client has confirmed a real amount; do not invent projected revenue.

## Email, WhatsApp, and AI writing help

- Use **Email inbox** to review connected mail and **Email outbox / Outreach & campaigns** to draft messages. Check recipients, language, amounts, dates, links, and claims before sending.
- AI-generated copy is a draft. Review and approve it before sending. Do not send outreach to people who opted out or are marked do-not-contact.
- The AI helper can help draft, proofread, translate, or summarize supported CRM records. It cannot send messages or update a client record just because you ask it to.

## Events and registrations

- Administrators create and manage events under **Events → Events & media**. Open an event to manage the public details, registration, speakers, agenda, gallery, flyer and QR code in one record. Check dates, venue, capacity, price, access and registration phase before publishing or sharing a poster.
- Use the private **Event plan for Joy** inside the event record for the client brief, lead, guest target, budget ceiling, contingency and planning notes. The plan and its client details are for authorized back-office users; they do not appear on the public event page.
- Use the readiness checklist to assign an owner, due date and status. Mark blocked tasks as blocked and record decisions or supplier changes in the plan.
- Add budget estimates by category (for example venue, catering, speaker, production, travel, marketing or livestream). Record the supplier, estimate and who pays. **Client pays supplier** is planning-only and stays out of Gotham's quote. **Gotham charges client** is eligible for a quote only when the line is priced and **Include in quote** is checked. **Included in service fee** is not added as a separate amount. Do not assume supplier availability, confirm bookings, or choose who pays without client agreement.
- **Save plan & prepare a quote** creates a draft from only the selected Gotham-billed lines. It does not issue or email it. Open the draft under **Sales → Invoices & quotes** and confirm the client, scope, amounts, tax treatment, dates and payment terms before issuing or sending it. Keep estimates clearly labelled until suppliers confirm them.
- **Registrations** lists attendee records; **Door check-in** is for validating attendees at the event. Do not share attendee lists outside the authorized team.

## Quotes and invoices

- Administrators create quotes and invoices under **Sales → Invoices & quotes**. Confirm the client, scope, agreed price, currency, and payment instructions before issuing anything.
- If a price or payment instruction is unclear, pause and ask the owner. Never invent bank or MoMo details.

## If something goes wrong

- Refresh the page once and check whether your account role provides access. If an error continues, note the screen name, the action you took, and the exact error message, then contact the owner.
- Never share a password, one-time code, session link, or customer financial credential with the AI helper or another user.
- For legal, tax, immigration, licensing, or investment questions, use the CRM only to record the question and next action. Ask a supervisor to verify the answer with the relevant authority or qualified professional.
