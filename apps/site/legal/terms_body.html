  • Mobile money and bank charges are paid by the sender.
  • Our fees are for preparation, advice and delivery. We never charge for access to a public official, and no part of any fee is a payment to influence a decision.

  • Memberships run month to month and may be cancelled any time before the next renewal. We do not refund part months.
  • Advisory work: a briefing fee is refundable until the briefing takes place. For project work, fees for work already done are not refundable, and your proposal sets out the stages.
