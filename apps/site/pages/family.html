Every yearin their birth month

From April 2026, every SSNIT pensioner must renew once a year. Miss it and pension payments can stop.

Renewal happens in the pensioner's birth month: at a branch, a partner bank, through the SSNIT app with face verification, or by home verification for people who can't travel. Your parent has to take part in person, so it can't be done from London or Houston. We make sure it happens, every year, on time.

Source: SSNIT, December 2025

Questions families ask

Can you do my father's SSNIT renewal for him?

Not on his behalf: SSNIT verifies the pensioner in person, or by face on the app. What we do is make sure it happens. We track his birth month, remind him, and either take him to a branch or partner bank, book SSNIT's home verification if he can't travel, or sit with him while he does it on the app.

My mother is over 70. Does she still need to renew NHIS?

Yes. People over 70 pay no NHIS premium, but membership still has to be renewed every year. It's one of the most common things that lapses without anyone noticing, and we handle it.

Do you provide nursing or personal care?

No, and we think that's the honest answer. Where your parent needs a nurse or a carer, we arrange one through a licensed partner, check their registration with the Nursing and Midwifery Council, and keep an eye on the care through our own visits.

What if my parent doesn't want this?

Then we don't do it. We always speak to your parent directly, in their language, before anything starts. It's their home and their decision. Many people agree once they understand we're there to help the family, not to replace it.

Which languages do your visitors speak?

We'll match the visitor to your parent's language, starting with Twi, Fante, Ga and Ewe in Greater Accra. Tell us on the form which language they're most comfortable in. Reports to you are in English.

My parent lives outside Accra. Can you help?

We're starting in Greater Accra. Join the list anyway and tell us where they are: we'll tell you honestly whether we can help yet, and the list shows us where to grow next.

How do I pay from abroad?

By card, in Ghana cedis. Ghanaian law doesn't allow us to price or charge in dollars, pounds or euros, so your bank converts the amount at its rate. Family members in Ghana can pay by mobile money.
