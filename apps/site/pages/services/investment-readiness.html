Questions

Good to know.

Can you guarantee we get the funding?

No. We prepare you so that you qualify and your application is complete and credible. The decision always rests with the programme, bank or investor.

How long does GIPA registration take?

Section 34 of Act 1173 says GIPA shall register a foreign-owned enterprise within five days of receiving a completed registration form if the statutory requirements are met. A foreign-owned enterprise registered under section 34 renews annually. Wholly Ghanaian-owned enterprises may register voluntarily and renew annually if they do.

Is there still a minimum capital requirement?

Not in general. Since 15 July 2026 most foreign-owned businesses have no minimum capital. Trading companies are the exception and need USD 500,000 in cash equity. We confirm the details for your case.
