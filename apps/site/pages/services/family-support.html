Questions

Good to know.

Which areas do you cover?

The Family & senior care plan covers Greater Accra. Other regions are available on request, so tell us where your family lives.

Do you provide medical care?

No. We visit, handle paperwork and help coordinate practical support. We are not a medical provider.

Who prepares the will?

Wills and estate paperwork are prepared with our partner lawyers. We gather the documents and keep you informed.

How do I pay from abroad?

Prices are fixed in Ghana cedis and paid by mobile money. If you live abroad, send us a WhatsApp message and we will agree the easiest way to pay.
