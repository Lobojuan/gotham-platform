Questions

Good to know.

How long does a concession screening take?

You receive a written report in 3 to 4 weeks.

Can foreign investors hold small-scale mining licences?

No. Small-scale licences are reserved for Ghanaian citizens, so foreign capital enters through large-scale licences, joint ventures or services. We explain the options in a briefing.

How do you handle the pending royalty reform?

A bill before Parliament would change gold royalties to a price-linked sliding scale. Until it is settled, we model both regimes for your project.

Do you charge for access to officials or approvals?

Never. We work through the formal application and consultation processes. Fees are for preparation and delivery, and any personal connection of our team to a public office is disclosed.
