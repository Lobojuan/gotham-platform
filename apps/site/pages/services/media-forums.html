How do I pay for a forum ticket?

By mobile money (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) to +233 55 041 6627, using the price and reference shown when you register. Then send us the transaction ID.

When is my seat confirmed?

Only after our staff have checked that the payment has arrived. Invitation-only forums confirm the invitation after review.

Can members get a discount?

Yes. Members get 20% off all forums. The free Ghana Business Brief on WhatsApp also announces upcoming forums.

Can my company sponsor a forum?

Yes. There are headline, partner and supporting options, and a directory listing across the site. Send us a WhatsApp message and we will share what each includes.
