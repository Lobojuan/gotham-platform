Questions

Good to know.

Is AI only for big companies?

No. Small businesses often gain the most, because a few hours saved each week on bookkeeping or messages matters. The readiness audit starts with what you already do.

Will AI replace our bookkeeper or accountant?

No. It prepares and sorts the records so your people spend less time typing and more time checking. A person still reviews the numbers.

What about our customers' data?

The readiness audit looks at what data would go where before anything is set up. For formal data protection work, see our Compliance & ESG practice.
