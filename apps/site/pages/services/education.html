Questions

Good to know.

Can you guarantee a licence or a scholarship?

No. We prepare complete, well-organised applications. Licences are decided by the regulator and scholarships by the institutions that award them.

Are government fees included?

No. Government and regulator fees are paid on top of our fixed price in Ghana cedis.

How long does licensing take?

It depends on the type of school and the regulator's process. Send us a WhatsApp message with your plans and we will explain the steps.

How do I pay?

You agree a fixed price in Ghana cedis before work starts and pay by mobile money.
