Field intelligenceTERRA

Pilot phase 2026

Our voice-first Android app for geological field work. Field teams capture readings by speaking, even offline, and every observation is checked on the spot.

  • GPS position with a stated accuracy tier, calibrated photos and a timestamp on every reading
  • Sensor kits from village-scale to geologist-grade, linked by USB-C or Bluetooth
  • Review queue and audit trail, so each data point can be traced
  • Supports JORC Table 1 evidence capture; a Competent Person still signs off

Geospatial platformAfri-MINEX

Built · 134 automated tests

Our secure web platform that maps concessions, licence boundaries and field data in one place, so investors, partners and reviewers look at the same record.

  • Concession and licence overlays with site data from TERRA
  • Multi-factor sign-in and cryptographically signed documents
  • Structured logs of who viewed or changed what, and when
  • Provenance records for buyers and responsible-sourcing checks

About Gotham Consult

A bridge between business, government and global partners.

Gotham Consult is an Accra-based business development and international projects consultancy, founded in 2018. We facilitate investment between the private sector and governments across Ghana, Nigeria, the UK, the US and Europe, combining business advisory, technology and media.

Joy Anthony Preston, MSc Strategic Leadership and Governance, leads the firm. She brings a decade of experience facilitating high-value partnerships, founded the Global Genius Foundation and convenes the Gotham Consult Business Seminar. She works with a team of senior consultants in finance, energy, mining and technology.

Strategic partner: Carlson Capital Ltd. Gotham Consult delivers mining, technology, fintech and investor services together with Carlson Capital Ltd, a company registered in Ghana. Carlson Capital brings field geology, in-house software platforms (TERRA, Afri-MINEX and Universal Credit Hub) and 25 years of project delivery across Africa and Europe.
