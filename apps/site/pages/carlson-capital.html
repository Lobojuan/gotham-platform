What Carlson Capital does, with Gotham

Four things, done on the ground.

Company set-up for large companies

For groups entering an African country: the legal entity, licences and approvals, local partners, recruiting the founding team, the first contracts and the systems to run it. Done before in DR Congo, Cameroon, Tanzania and other African markets.

AI, software & apps

Platforms built with AI and run for the client: credit and financial data (Universal Credit Hub, for 54 African countries), payment-ready websites, and this website and back office.

Mining

Geology-led screening and due diligence, and mine-site power, for investors and concession holders who need facts they can check. We know every formal route to get mining work done in Ghana, from first licence to production, and we work through it for you.

Energy projects

Utility-scale solar from permits and grid to EPC, power purchase agreement and financing. See the 130 MW Dawa case study.
