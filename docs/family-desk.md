# Family Desk — research and launch plan

Gotham Consult's service for older people in Ghana, paid for mostly by their
children abroad. Working name: **Family Desk**.

Researched 25 September 2026. Every claim below carries its source. Where the
research could not confirm something, it says so — act on the gaps before
acting on the claims.

---

## 1. The offer in one line

> We look after the paperwork, the house and the check-ins for your parent in
> Ghana, and you get a written report you can trust.

It is a **family administration and welfare** service. It is not a nursing
agency. That line is what makes it lawful to run without a health licence,
what separates it from every competitor, and what keeps the risk manageable.

## 2. Why now

**The SSNIT rule.** From April 2026 every SSNIT pensioner must renew their
pensioner certificate once a year, in their birth month. Miss it and they risk
being removed from the pension payroll. Channels: SSNIT app with face
verification, Virtual Branch, selected GCB and Ecobank branches, 53 SSNIT
branches, or home verification for pensioners with mobility problems, booked
through the Contact Centre on 0302 611 622.
[SSNIT, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.ssnit.org.gh/ssnit-implements-new-annual-pensioner-certificate-renewal-policy-for-all-pensioners-2/) ·
[Graphic, 12 Dec 2025](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-pensioners-must-self-validate-annually-to-guarantee-pay-ssnit.html) ·
[3News, 17 Sep 2026](https://3news.com/news/ssnit-urges-pensioners-to-renew-their-certificates-annually-or-risk-being-deactivated)

**The money is already moving.** Remittances to Ghana were about US$7.8bn in
2025, above foreign direct investment, with the US the largest source.
[Citi Newsroom, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/04/bog-governor-remittance-inflows-near-7-8bn-in-2025-surpass-fdi/)

**The people.** 1,991,736 Ghanaians are aged 60+, and **341,960 of them live
alone** — 62,480 of those are 80+.
[GSS 2021 Census, 1 Oct 2022](https://census2021.statsghana.gov.gh/presspage.php?readmorenews=MTc2ODIyNTE2NS4yMjU1)

**The payers.** About 1.06m Ghana-born people live abroad: US 258,000, UK
155,000, Italy 49,000, Canada 26,000, Spain 24,000. These are foreign-born
counts; the second generation is extra.
[MPI / UN DESA 2024, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/journal/country-profile/ghana-migration-trends)

## 3. What we do, and what the law says about each

### Renewals and paperwork

| Task | How it actually works | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **SSNIT pensioner renewal** | Annual, birth month. App uses face verification, so the pensioner must be present. Home verification exists for the housebound. | Track the month, send the reminder, book home verification or take them to a branch or partner bank, or sit with them through the app. **We do not renew on their behalf** — no official source says an agent can. |
| **NHIS** | 12-month membership, renewed yearly by *929#, MyNHIS app or district office. Over-70s pay no premium but **must still renew**. [Pulse, 26 Mar 2026](https://www.pulse.com.gh/story/how-to-register-for-nhis-in-ghana-in-2026-full-guide-2026032613421687997) | Renew by *929# with their card number and mobile money. Unconfirmed officially that a relative may do this — ask NHIA. |
| **Ghana Card** | Valid 10 years. Biometrics in person; **no proxy**. Mobile units for disabled or hospitalised people by arrangement; household mobile registration needs 7+ applicants at GH¢300 each. Replacement GH¢200 at a district office. [NIA FAQ](https://register.nia.gov.gh/faqs) · [NIA fees 2026](https://nia.gov.gh/fees-and-charges-2026/) | Accompany them, or arrange the mobile unit. |
| **LEAP** | Cash grant for people 65+ without family support: GH¢320–530 every two months. [GNA, 1 Jul 2025](https://gna.org.gh/2025/07/government-increases-leap-grant-for-beneficiaries-effective-june-30/) | Check eligibility where relevant. |
| **Property rates** | Over-70s are said to be exempt, but assemblies apply it inconsistently. [Graphic, 29 Nov 2025](https://www.graphic.com.gh/features/reality-zone/ghana-news-aged-persons-bill-aged-lives-matter.html) | Claim it where it applies. |

### Wills, estates and authority — through a partner lawyer

- **Wills Act 1971 (Act 360):** in writing, signed before two witnesses present
  together. For a blind or illiterate person it must be read over and explained,
  with a declaration on the will. A gift to a witness is void. A sealed will can
  be deposited with the High Court in the person's lifetime.
  [Wills Act](https://judicial.gov.gh/jsweb/acts/willsact.pdf)
- **No will:** PNDCL 111 applies. The spouse and children take the house and
  household chattels; the rest splits spouse 3/16, children 9/16, surviving
  parent 1/8, customary family 1/8. The reform bill has not been enacted.
  [Dennis Law News, 21 Jun 2026](https://dennislawnews.com/opinions-and-features-v2/intestate-succession-in-ghana-how-pndcl-111-shapes-families-after-death)
- **Power of attorney (Act 549): it ends automatically on death or loss of
  mental capacity.** Ghana has no lasting or enduring power of attorney. A family
  that wants someone to act for a parent must set it up while the parent is fully
  capable — and it still fails if dementia sets in. This is one of the most
  valuable things to tell a family early.
  [Ghana Law Hub](https://ghanalawhub.com/powers-of-attorney-what-does-ghana-law-say-2/)
- A power of attorney over land must be registered at the Land Registration
  Division (Land Act 2020, around s.170 — check the section before quoting it).

### Property and land watch

- Land guards face 5–15 years in prison under the Land Act 2020.
  [Citi Newsroom, Sep 2021](https://citinewsroom.com/2021/09/land-guards-to-serve-5-to-15-years-in-jail-lands-commission/)
- Official searches go through the Lands Commission, including an online
  consolidated search. [Lands Commission](https://www.lc.gov.gh/services/official-administrative-search/)
- The Bank of Ghana Governor has warned of syndicates pledging property as loan
  security with forged titles, without the owners knowing.
  [17 Jun 2026](https://inghananewstoday.com/2026/06/17/land-documents-loan-collateral-risk-financial-sector-bog-governor/)
- NGO guidance for diaspora owners: fence the land, keep a regular watch, and be
  wary of relatives or middlemen selling without authority.
  [Diaspora Affairs GH, 25 Sep 2025](https://diasporaaffairsgh.org/how-to-avoid-land-disputes-in-ghana-diasporans/)

**What we sell:** scheduled visits with geotagged photos, an annual official
search, and a written report. Disputes go to the partner lawyer.

### Care — the line we do not cross

- **HeFRA (Act 829)** licenses *facilities*, defined as premises. It is silent on
  care in people's homes, and its licence categories include "Nursing Home" but
  no home-care agency category.
  [Act 829](https://www.brr.gov.gh/acc/registry/docs/HEALTH%20INSTITUTIONS%20AND%20FACILITIES%20ACT,%202011%20(ACT%20829).pdf) ·
  [HeFRA](https://hefra.gov.gh/license-requirement/)
- **Nursing and Midwifery Council** registers nurses and nurse assistants and
  offers licence verification.
  [NMC](https://www.nmc.gov.gh/web/our-services)
- **No regulator** was found for non-medical welfare visits.
- An academic view: Accra's commercial home-care agencies are "completely
  unregulated". [Coe, The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/many-old-people-in-ghana-need-daily-care-how-the-government-can-help-152767)

**So:** welfare visits and paperwork are ours. Any nursing or personal care is
arranged through a partner, and every nurse is checked against the NMC register
before they set foot in a client's home. **Before launch, ask HeFRA in writing
whether coordinating home care needs a licence** — the Act is silent, and silence
is not permission.

## 4. Data protection — the parent is the client

- Health is **special personal data** under Act 843 s.96. Processing it needs
  consent or necessity (s.37).
- s.21: data must be collected **from the person it is about**, unless they
  consent otherwise.
- The practical rule: **the parent consents, not the child.** A daughter in London
  can start the conversation, but her mother agrees to the visits and to her
  information being kept — in her own language, before anything is recorded.
- The website interest form therefore collects **no health information and no
  name for the parent** — only their town, their language and what kind of help
  the family is after.
- The DPC registration must cover this processing.
  [Act 843](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2012/843/eng@2012-05-18)

## 5. Payments — the rule that shapes pricing

**The Bank of Ghana prohibits pricing, advertising and receiving payment for
goods and services in foreign currency in Ghana.** Foreign-currency payments
between residents and non-residents must go through a bank (Foreign Exchange
Act 2006, s.15).
[BoG notice](https://www.bog.gov.gh/notice/prohibition-of-pricing-and-payment-for-goods-and-services-in-foreign-currency/) ·
[Act 723](https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2006/723/eng@2006-12-29)

- **Paystack Ghana** accepts international cards but charges and settles them in
  **cedis**. Only Kenyan and Nigerian businesses can take USD. Fee 1.95%.
  Subscriptions work on cards.
  [Paystack pricing](https://paystack.com/gh/pricing) · [USD support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130690)
- **Stripe** is not available to Ghanaian businesses.
- **Flutterwave** takes international cards at 4.8%.

**Decision:** Family Desk is priced in **Ghana cedis only**. A family abroad pays
by card; their bank converts. Family members in Ghana can pay by mobile money.
No USD, GBP or EUR prices anywhere — on the site, in emails or in proposals.
If you ever want to price in foreign currency, that needs an offshore entity and
written advice from a bank or lawyer first.

## 7. Price anchors (you set the final numbers)

- Accra households say they would pay on average **GH¢1,403 a month** for formal
  elder care (range GH¢300–3,000). Existing operators charge **GH¢500–1,500 a
  month**. [Springer, 27 Sep 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12982-024-00231-9)
- AgedCare Ghana: from **GH¢150 per visit**.
- Caregiver gross pay: **GH¢1,288–4,045 a month** (middle 80%).
  [Paylab](https://www.paylab.com/gh/salaryinfo/medicine-social-care/caregiver)

A reasonable starting shape, to test in the pilot, not to publish yet:

| Plan | What's in it | Where it might sit |
|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | Renewals calendar (SSNIT, NHIS, Ghana Card), monthly phone check-in in their language, SSNIT renewal help | Low hundreds of cedis a month |
| **Visit** | Essentials + one home visit a month, photo and written report | Around the GH¢500–800 band |
| **Full** | Fortnightly visits, property watch with annual search, appointment escort, first call in an emergency | Near the GH¢1,400 households said they'd pay |

Measure the real cost of a visit in the pilot — in Accra traffic it is often
half a working day — and price at roughly three times direct cost.

## 8. How to earn trust

Families abroad have been burned before, often by relatives. The research is
consistent on what they want:

- **Proof, not promises:** geotagged photos, a written report after every visit,
  video calls on request. [GhanaWeb, 15 Jun 2025](https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/Investing-in-Real-Estate-from-Abroad-A-must-read-guide-for-Ghana-s-diaspora-1987917)
- **Vetted staff:** Ghana Card checked, police clearance (the candidate applies
  at [eservices.police.gov.gh](https://eservices.police.gov.gh), about 10 working
  days), references called.
  [Pulse, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.pulse.com.gh/story/ghana-police-clearance-certificate-guide-2026080416044931142)
- **Money rules:** no staff member holds a client's cash or original documents
  alone. Every expense receipted.
- **The same visitor each time,** introduced by name and photo.

Competitors already sell GPS tracking and one-hour photo reports. That is the
table stake, not the differentiator.

## 9. Culture — get this right or nothing else matters

- **Frame it as support for the family, not a replacement.** Old-people's homes
  are stigmatised as abandonment.
  [Dovie, Frontiers 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fsoc.2019.00025/full)
- **Respect:** Papa, Mama, Nana. Greet the most senior person first. Right hand;
  both hands when handing things over.
- **Language:** 30.2% of people 6+ cannot read or write; 87.9% literacy in Greater
  Accra, 32.8% in Savannah. Visits happen in the parent's language; the report
  to the family is in English. [GSS 2021](https://census2021.statsghana.gov.gh/)
- **Faith:** 71.3% Christian, 19.9% Muslim. Plan around church, prayer times and
  Ramadan; offer a same-sex visitor where asked.
- **Witchcraft accusations** target poor older women. Treat any sign as a
  safeguarding matter. [Amnesty, Jul 2026](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/07/ghana-witchcraft-akua/)
- **Emergencies:** only 19% of facilities have a dedicated 24-hour emergency
  unit. We never promise emergency medical response — we promise to be the first
  call, get there, and keep the family informed.
  [Citi Newsroom, 19 Feb 2026](https://citinewsroom.com/2026/02/beyond-the-bed-the-crisis-behind-ghanas-emergency-care-failures/)

## 10. Where the customers are

Google search volume for "caring for elderly parents in Ghana" is too low to
register, and "Accra home care" is taken by a US company. **This is not an SEO
business.** Families ask in churches, associations and WhatsApp groups.

- **US:** NCOGA (New York tri-state), COGA (Washington DC area), Ghana National
  Council of Chicago, Ghana Council of Georgia. The Bronx has the largest
  concentration.
- **UK:** National Congress of Ghanaian Unions (London), Ghana Union Midlands,
  Asanteman UK; London, Manchester, Birmingham, Milton Keynes.
- **Churches:** Church of Pentecost USA (about 75 districts, strongest in NY,
  PA/NJ, TX); Presbyterian Church of Ghana North America (12 districts).
- **Old students:** Old Achimotan Association UK, Mfantsipim Old Boys USA.
- **Media:** GhanaWeb diaspora pages, New York Ghana Radio, One Ghana Radio UK.
- **Government:** the Diaspora Affairs Office at the Presidency (not the private
  NGO with a similar name).

One pastor mentioning Family Desk from the pulpit is worth more than any ad.

## 12. Could not confirm — resolve before launch

- Whether a relative or agent can do the SSNIT renewal, and whether arrears are
  paid after deactivation. **Ask SSNIT.**
- Whether one person can renew another's NHIS officially, and the 70+ fee.
  **Ask NHIA.**
- Whether HeFRA treats home-care coordination as needing a licence. **Ask HeFRA
  in writing.**
- Whether NIA will send a mobile unit for a single housebound person.
- The exact Land Act section numbers on registering a power of attorney.
- Whether the Data Protection Bill 2025 has been enacted.
- Current police clearance fee.
- Any exemption allowing a Ghanaian company to take foreign currency for services
  sold to non-residents. **Assume none.**
