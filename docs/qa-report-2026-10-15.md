# Production readiness QA — 15 October 2026

## Decision

**Application smoke checks passed; production readiness is conditional, not certified.** The current site and CRM are online and the requested API/browser suites pass. Several operational controls in SRS section 7 still lack evidence, so this audit does not certify the service for unrestricted production use.

## Evidence

- Source revision audited: `83fa77a4b8c9d4f3e69669358267a74a0d8a5474` (`origin/main` at audit start).
- `bash tests/run_api.sh`: 40 suites passed, 0 failed. Coverage includes auth and roles, registration, CRM records and workflows, calendar, invoices/settings, public enquiries, Q&A/chat/voice, sourced data, SEO, and email inbox/outbox behavior.
- `bash tests/e2e/run.sh`: CRM desktop and phone journeys passed; 18,073 site checks passed across 182 routes and six device sizes; event-help form flow passed; six-language voice and locale checks passed; investor portal, report download, touch target and user-management/mobile-role suites passed.
- Event-help browser checks confirmed consent is required before sending; the form reports a reference only after a successful CRM response; confirmation describes queued email/alerts accurately; WhatsApp is optional and user-initiated; failed submissions remain retryable.
- Live read-only smoke on 15 Oct: `/`, `/register`, `/admin`, `/event-help`, `/invest`, `/sources`, `/ar`, `/zh` returned HTTP 200; `/api/health` returned `{"ok":true,"database":"ok"}`.
- No production registrations or enquiries were submitted and no email was sent as part of this audit.

## Open production-readiness evidence

SRS section 7 remains not certified. Close these before describing the whole operation as production-ready:

1. **O-1 Backups:** verify scheduled backups, encryption, retention, off-site copy, and a restore rehearsal on the actual host.
2. **O-2 Monitoring:** verify the deployed monitor checks database, disk and email delivery and successfully alerts the owner.
3. **O-3 Rollback:** rehearse a release rollback while retaining registrations and media written after that release.
4. **O-5 Data lifecycle:** approve and test retention, export, correction and deletion handling for client contacts, event registrations and consent.
5. **O-6 Auditability:** review whether sensitive admin actions across accounts, payments, contacts, campaigns and deals are attributable and reviewable.
6. **O-7 Accessibility:** complete keyboard, screen reader, contrast and reduced-motion checks, including a real iPhone/Safari pass. Current responsive coverage uses Chromium emulation.

Email confirmations and staff alerts are queued in the CRM. A queued message is not proof of delivery; an owner should review the outbox/transport and verify delivery with a controlled mailbox check before relying on client notifications.

## Readiness call

The core journeys are suitable for a controlled public test. Do not claim full production certification until the listed operational evidence is recorded. The static SRS does not by itself establish that host-side backups, alerts, email transport or rollback have been exercised.
